Play Battle Network netbattles online.

nettai is rollback netplay for Mega Man Battle Network 4, 5 and 6. Make a build, then play a friend online, practice, or watch your old matches.

Download
  • Battle Network 4Red Sun and Blue Moon
  • Battle Network 5Team ProtoMan and Team Colonel
  • Battle Network 6Cybeast Gregar and Cybeast Falzar

Online

Make a room and send the code to a friend. They type it in and you're battling.

Builds

Choose your navi, put a folder together, and set up the NaviCust and patch cards.

Practice

Practice against a dummy. It can stand still or fight back.

Replays

Your matches are saved, so you can watch them again or turn them into videos.
